About me

I'm an IB-certified MYP/DP Design and Technology facilitator with a B.E. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering, currently pursuing an M.Tech in Design Engineering at BITS Pilani. I bring over 7 years of experience across engineering, industry, and education.

I'm passionate about bringing technology, creativity, and problem-solving together. I design meaningful, inquiry-based learning experiences that help students think critically and connect what they learn to real-world applications.

My hands-on expertise spans woodworking, additive manufacturing, CAD modelling, finite element analysis, mechanism design, and electronics, applied both in engineering practice and in the Makerspace classroom.
